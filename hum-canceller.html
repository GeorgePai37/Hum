Hum Canceller

Experimental. Cancels one steady low hum (engine, fan, AC) by playing its mirror image through your headphones.

-- Hz
Lock frequency
How to tune
  1. Put on your headphones, keep the phone near you, tap Start.
  2. Wait until the Hz reading stays steady, then turn on Lock.
  3. Raise Strength slowly, then slide Phase until the hum is quietest.
  4. Re-tune Phase if the hum comes back.

Works only on one constant tone. Voices, traffic and changing sounds are not cancelled. Bluetooth delay is absorbed by the Phase slider. Keep volume low: a wrong setting can make the hum louder. On iPhones, connected headphones may switch to a lower-quality mic mode while this runs.
